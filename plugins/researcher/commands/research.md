# Research Command

Research the requested topic using an evidence-first workflow.

## Prompt

```text
Research the question or topic I provide. Define the scope, gather reliable sources, compare evidence, cite claims, and summarize what is known, uncertain, or disputed.
```

## Expected Inputs

- A research question, claim, topic, product, policy, paper, market, or technical decision.
- Optional constraints such as timeframe, geography, audience, source type, or output format.

## Expected Output

- A concise answer.
- Evidence with citations tied to specific claims.
- Caveats for uncertainty, conflicting sources, source gaps, or stale information risk.
